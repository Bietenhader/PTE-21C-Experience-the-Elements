# Layouts

Four layouts, generated by `tools/build_layouts.py` into `public/data/layouts/`, all in one 32-column
coordinate space so switching between them is a pure translation of tiles (SPEC §7.2). Every position follows
from the Madelung filling order: the electron that completes element Z enters subshell (n, l) as the k-th
electron of that subshell. Since M1 the app switches between them with a FLIP morph: each tile flies from
its old cell to its new one (600 ms, 8 ms stagger by Z) while the row and column labels cross-fade.

| Layout | Rows | Columns | Group-3 toggle |
|---|---|---|---|
| `standard18` | periods 1–7, a gap, then two detached f rows | groups 1–18 at columns 8–25; f-cells 1–15 under groups 3–17 | moves exactly La, Lu, Ac, Lr |
| `long32` | periods 1–7 | s 1–2, f 3–16 inline, d and p 17–32 | swaps La↔Lu and Ac↔Lr; Ce–Yb and Th–No shift one column |
| `janet` | n + l = 1 … 8 | f 1–14, d 15–24, p 25–30, s 31–32 | none (Lu/Lr start the d-block) |
| `adomah` | n = 1 (bottom) … 7 (top) | as `janet` | none |

- **Default group 3:** Lu and Lr under Sc and Y (IUPAC 2021), variant `lu`; La and Ac is variant `la`.
- **Janet** row lengths are 2, 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 30 — the last row reaches 32 with elements 119 and 120
  (v0.2). The layout deliberately ignores the measured anomalies; its caption says so.
- **Long form with La and Ac in group 3.** As SPEC §7.2 prescribes, the inline block stays 14 cells wide and
  the group-3 column stays to its right, so the block holds Ce–Lu and Th–Lr and lanthanum and actinium stand
  after them. The caption says so. (Many printed long forms instead move the group-3 column to the left of
  the block; see STATUS.md.)
- **Markers.** In `standard18` an asterisk sits at the left edge of the cell where the detached row belongs
  (between Ba and Lu with the default variant) and at the start of the detached row itself.

Tests (`tools/tests/test_layouts.py`): every element exactly once in every layout and variant; Janet row
lengths and rows = n + l; ADOMAH rows by n with n = 1 at the bottom; the toggle behaviour above, with every
other tile keeping its relative order.
