Darmstadtium is a synthetic element in group 10, below platinum, named after the German city of Darmstadt, where the GSI laboratory detected its first atom on 9 November 1994. Producing that atom meant firing at least a billion billion nickel-62 ions at a lead-208 target over about a week; the product, darmstadtium-269, has a half-life of about 0.2 milliseconds. Heavier isotopes made since last longer, darmstadtium-281 about 12 seconds, but only small numbers of atoms have ever existed. Calculations predict the configuration [Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d⁸ 7s², unlike platinum's 5d⁹ 6s¹: relativity draws the 7s orbital inwards and lowers its energy, so it keeps both electrons. None of this has been measured.
