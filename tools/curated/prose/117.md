Tennessine is a synthetic element in group 17, below astatine, named after the US state of Tennessee, home of Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Vanderbilt University and the University of Tennessee. Its discovery, announced in April 2010 by a Russian–American team at Dubna, depended on a rare target: berkelium-249 bred in a reactor at Oak Ridge. Because that berkelium decays with a half-life of 330 days, it had to be shipped and bombarded with calcium-48 promptly. Only a few atoms resulted, and its two known isotopes have half-lives of only about 21 and 51 milliseconds. Calculations predict [Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7s² 7p⁵, and strong spin–orbit effects should make it less like a typical halogen than iodine is.
