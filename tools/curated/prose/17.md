Chlorine is a greenish-yellow, choking gas from the halogen group; we meet it bound and harmless in table salt, while small doses of chlorine or its compounds keep drinking water and swimming pools free of microbes. Natural chlorine is a mixture of two stable isotopes, about 75.8 % chlorine-35 and 24.2 % chlorine-37, which is why its atomic weight, about 35.45, is so far from a whole number. The atom, [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ (ground term ²P°₃/₂), lacks one electron to close its shell, and it releases more energy on capturing an extra electron than any other element, even fluorine.
