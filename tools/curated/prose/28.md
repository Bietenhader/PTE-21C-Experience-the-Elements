Nickel is a silvery, corrosion-resistant metal used mainly in stainless steel, as well as in coins and rechargeable batteries; it also forms, with iron, the metal of most iron meteorites. Plants need a trace of it for urease, the enzyme that breaks down urea. In the nucleus, nickel holds a record: nickel-62 has the highest binding energy per nucleon of any nucleus, about 8.79 MeV, slightly more than iron-56. The free atom's ground configuration is [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s² (term ³F₄), but a level from the rival arrangement 3d⁹ 4s¹ lies only 0.025 eV above it, so the two are almost tied.
